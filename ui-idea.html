Design philosophy

Why Vault OS
feels different.

01

Access is not a feature — it's the foundation

Most tools bolt on permissions late. We built the role engine first, then the product around it.

02

Reading is a practice, not a transaction

Streaks, mastery scores, and revision schedules exist because knowledge compounds when you return to it.

03

One reader at the center

No public profile. No feed. No notifications. Vault is optimized for the depth of one person's thinking.

04

Everything is auditable, nothing is opaque

Every action — read, edit, permission change — is timestamped. You always know what happened and who did it.
